### Power BI Dashboard | Customer Behaviour & RFM Segmentation

**Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) • Customer & Transaction Frequency Analysis • RFM Customer Segmentation**

## Project Overview

This project analyses e-commerce sales and customer transaction data to evaluate overall business performance, understand purchasing behaviour, and identify customer value.

The Power BI dashboard integrates Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), Customer & Transaction Frequency Analysis, and RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) Customer Segmentation to transform transactional data into meaningful business insights.

The analysis focuses on understanding sales patterns, customer purchasing frequency, revenue contribution, and customer segments to identify opportunities for customer retention, re-engagement, and business growth.

## Analytical Summary

### 1. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

- Examined overall sales performance across countries, products, and time periods.
- Identified differences in quantity sold and sales contribution across products and markets.
- Analysed time-based and geographical sales patterns to establish overall business performance.

### 2. Customer & Transaction Frequency Analysis

- Analysed customer transaction frequency and revenue contribution.
- Compared purchasing frequency with customer revenue and average purchase value.
- Examined customer-level sales distribution to distinguish frequent purchasers from higher-revenue customers.
- Identified customer groups requiring different engagement and retention strategies.

### 3. Customer Segmentation — RFM Analysis

- Applied Recency, Frequency, and Monetary (RFM) analysis to evaluate customer purchasing behaviour and value.
- Segmented customers into High Value, Medium Value, Low Value, and Dormant groups.
- Compared customer distribution and revenue contribution across segments.
- Evaluated RFM scores and customer activity trends to understand engagement and customer value.

## Key Findings

- Customer purchasing behaviour varies significantly across the customer base.
- Customers with higher transaction frequency generally tend to generate higher cumulative revenue, although some customers generate significant revenue from fewer purchases.
- Revenue contribution differs across customer segments, highlighting the importance of customer-level segmentation.
- Low-value and dormant customers represent opportunities for targeted re-engagement.
- Higher-value customers are important for retention and long-term customer value.
- Sales patterns vary across products, countries, and time periods, providing additional context for understanding business performance.

## Business Questions Answered

**What is happening in the business?**

EDA identifies overall sales performance, product contribution, geographical distribution, and time-based trends.

**How do customers purchase?**

Transaction frequency analysis examines how often customers transact and how purchasing behaviour relates to revenue generation.

**Which customers are most valuable?**

RFM analysis identifies customer groups based on Recency, Frequency, and Monetary contribution.

**Where are the business opportunities?**

Customer segmentation highlights opportunities for customer retention, dormant customer re-engagement, and customer value growth.
